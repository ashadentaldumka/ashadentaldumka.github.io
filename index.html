Frequently asked questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. We offer painless RCT in Dumka: treatment is done under local anaesthesia with advanced rotary instruments, so most patients feel little or no pain during the procedure. It relieves the toothache caused by the infection.

Can root canal treatment be done in a single sitting?

Yes. Asha Dental Clinic offers single-sitting RCT in Dumka using advanced rotary instruments. Whether your tooth can be treated in one visit depends on the infection and the tooth, which Dr. Prasenjeet checks before treatment.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with one or more missing teeth and healthy gums can get dental implants. Dr. Prasenjeet checks your bone and gum health at a consultation before planning treatment.
